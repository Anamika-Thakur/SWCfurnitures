Solid Wood vs. Veneer vs. Laminate

Choosing honestly, for your project.

1

Solid Wood

The most durable and premium option. Best for statement furniture that should last decades.

2

Veneer

A thin layer of real wood over a stable core — the look of solid wood at a lower cost.

3

Laminate

Practical, easy to maintain, and available in a wide range of finishes — a strong everyday choice.
