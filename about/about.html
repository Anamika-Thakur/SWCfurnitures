Two generations at the workbench.

SWCfurnitures began as a small family workshop and has grown into a team of in-house carpenters — trusted equally by homeowners furnishing a single room and builders fitting out entire towers.

Our Story

From one workshop to city-wide contracts.

1999

A single workshop

Founded as a small carpentry shop taking custom furniture orders in the neighbourhood.

2005

Became our customers' first choice

Built a reputation for dependable quality, timely execution and craftsmanship that turned first-time customers into long-term clients.

2010

Expanded across Delhi, UP & Haryana

Growing demand took us beyond our neighbourhood, with larger residential and commercial projects across the region.

2026

5000+ projects and counting

Today, decades of experience and a dedicated team help us create quality furniture and woodwork solutions for homes, builders and businesses.

Values

What guides every project.
